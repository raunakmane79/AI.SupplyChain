OEE & Bottleneck Diagnostics

Enter basic line data — planned time, downtime, output, and ideal cycle time — to compute Availability, Performance, Quality, and OEE. ChainIQ interprets whether the primary loss driver is availability, speed, or quality.

Based on TPM OEE model OEE target: 85% "world class"

Definitions: Availability = operating time / planned time; Performance = (ideal cycle · total count) / operating time; Quality = good / total; OEE = A × P × Q.

ChainIQ Make Insight

Feed in your line data and ChainIQ will decompose OEE into its three loss buckets, highlighting whether downtime, speed loss, or scrap is your main improvement opportunity.

Interpretation guide

  • World-class OEE is often cited around 85% (A≈90%, P≈95%, Q≈99%).
  • Availability < 85% usually implies changeover, breakdown, or setup losses worth attacking with TPM and SMED.
  • Performance < 90% suggests micro-stops, slow cycles, or speed derating.
  • Quality < 99% points to scrap, rework, and yield issues at the bottleneck.
